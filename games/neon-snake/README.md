# Neon Snake 2084

A free browser game by Matthew "Frog" Finley. Watch AI snakes compete, or play against the bots with the keyboard, on-screen pad, or a swipe.

**Play it:** <https://m-fin.github.io/crave-lab/games/neon-snake/>

- **WATCH AI** — four bot brains play a seeded match.
- **YOU vs BOTS** — you are the white snake. Arrows or WASD, or swipe / the on-screen pad on a phone.
- `?mode=vs&seed=217` opens a specific match.

No install. The game is plain HTML and JavaScript.
